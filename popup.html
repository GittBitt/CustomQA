CustomQA

Welcome to CustomQA

This extension enhances your YouTube viewing experience with AI-powered Audio Descriptions and Visual Question Answering.

Audio Descriptions (AD)

The AD feature provides automatic audio descriptions for the video you are watching.

  1. Open the "Audio Descriptions" tab in the sidebar.
  2. Customize the description style using the available options (Length, Frequency, Emphasis, etc.).
  3. Click the "GENERATE AD" button. The extension will capture frames and generate descriptions.
  4. Once generated, the descriptions will appear in the display box and play automatically as the video progresses.
  5. You can manually play any description by clicking the speaker icon next to it.

Visual Question Answering (VQA)

The VQA feature allows you to ask questions about the video content.

  1. Open the "Visual Question Answer" tab in the sidebar.
  2. Type your question into the chat input box at the bottom.
  3. The extension will capture seconds around the current timestamp and use them to answer your question.
  4. The answer will appear in the chatbox and be read aloud.

Customization

Both AD and VQA features can be customized to your liking. Use the various sliders and buttons in the sidebar to adjust settings like voice gender, speed, and content style.
